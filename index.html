Core Features

WhatsApp Sales Logging

Simply send an item name, quantity, and price via WhatsApp to log a sale. HisaabPro does the rest.

Automated Inventory Tracking

Stock levels automatically decrease with each sale. Manage new stock and low alerts effortlessly.

Daily Profit/Loss Calculation

Understand your daily earnings by automatically subtracting item costs from sales revenue.

Daily Summary Report

Receive a concise end-of-day WhatsApp report detailing sales, profits, and low stock warnings.

Multi-User Access

Allow multiple employees to log sales under your store's account, keeping everything centralized.

Guided Setup & Management

Easy WhatsApp-based onboarding to set up items, cost prices, and manage your inventory.

Payment & History Tracking

Tag sales as cash or credit, view credit sales, and request historical data summaries.

Urdu/English Support

Interact with HisaabPro in the language you prefer, for seamless communication.

How It Works

1. Setup Items

Guide your initial inventory setup via a simple WhatsApp conversation, including item names and their cost prices.

2. Log Sales

As you make sales, send a quick WhatsApp message with item, quantity, and price. HisaabPro automatically updates inventory and records the sale.

3. Get Insights

Receive daily summary reports on sales, profits, and low stock alerts. Access past data and manage items anytime via WhatsApp.
